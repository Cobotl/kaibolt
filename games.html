Where are the games?

Cobolt for KaiOS (or Kaibolt for the official client) is not allowed to download games. The main issue with downloading games in KaiOS outside KaiStore (KaiOS app store) is that some devices are simply not allowed to sideload.

The device that was used for testing raw metal needs to use the KaiStore testing channel because the firmware is not allowed to, and so is probably your device. So, if you want the games, and probably not able to sideload them and just bloat your SD Card, please go to cobotl.github.io on the browser or simply use the computer you have.

Another reason is that no one is actually developing gaming for feature phones that doesn't support .jar and learning another language that isn't Java for newer phones is not necessary, and worse with a webapp, we accept .zip packages for downloads and .zip is also compatible with KaiOS sideloading (as long as it is a webapp).

In short. firmware and complications with compatibility.
